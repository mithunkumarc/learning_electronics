brushed motor can be ac or dc

- motor has two parts stator part and rotor part
- stator can be electromagnet or permanent magnet
- rotor can be electromagnet or permanent magnet
- stator and rotor both are like coil, maxwell law says coil acts as electromagnet when current pass through it.
- 
#### brushed ac motor, 
ac current itself reverse so rotor electromagnet poles changes automatically
#### brushed dc motor
- in dc motor, dc connected to rotor, rotor has circular ring spit into half, 
- one brush from battery connected to positive terminal and another brush connected to negative terminal
- in 0 - 180 degree rotation of rotor, dc current flow from fist plate to second plate become first North- second South electromagnet
- in 180 - 360 degree rotation of rotor, dc current flow from second plate to first plate become second North - first South electromagnet
- according to Fleming's left hand rule motor works
